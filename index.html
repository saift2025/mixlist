      Show this on your TV

      1. Put your phone and TV on the same Wi-Fi.

      2. Swipe down from the top of the screen and tap Cast, Smart View or Screen Cast (the name depends on your phone), then pick your TV.

      3. Play your playlist here. Whatever is on your phone screen shows on the TV, so YouTube and phone videos both work, and the playlist keeps moving to the next video. The screen stays awake while a video is playing.

      For a single YouTube video you can also try the cast icon inside the YouTube player, or tap ✏️ Edit and use "↗ YouTube" on that video to open it in the YouTube app.
